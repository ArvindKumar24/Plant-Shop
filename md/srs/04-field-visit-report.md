# 4. Field Visit Report

## 4.1 Details of Field Visit

### 4.1.1 Visit Summary

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Location** | GreenField Plant Nursery & Garden Centre, Local Market Road |
| **Date(s)** | 2 days of observation (a Saturday and a weekday) |
| **Purpose** | To understand the current process of buying and selling plants, gather customer behaviour data, and observe the manual order/inventory management system. |
| **Participants** | Shop owner, 2 staff members, and ~19 customers (interviewed on-site) |
| **Method** | Direct observation, semi-structured interviews, printed questionnaire distribution, and photographic documentation. |

### 4.1.2 Objectives of the Visit

1. To observe how customers browse and select plants in a physical store.
2. To understand the challenges faced by the shop owner in managing inventory and orders.
3. To collect quantitative survey responses from real customers.
4. To identify gaps that an online store could address.

### 4.1.3 What Was Observed

- The nursery displayed plants in **open racks**; customers inspected plants physically before buying.
- **Stock tracking was manual** — the owner used a written notebook to record sales and stock.
- Orders for specific plants were mostly handled **over phone/WhatsApp**, with no formal tracking of delivery status.
- Customers frequently asked staff about plant **care requirements** and **price**.
- Most transactions were **cash-based**, though many customers noted they use UPI apps regularly.
- The owner had difficulty estimating live stock and sometimes promised plants that were out of stock.

## 4.3 Analysis of Field Visit Findings

### 4.3.1 Key Observations Mapped to Requirements

| Observation | Resulting System Requirement |
|-------------|------------------------------|
| Manual notebook stock tracking | Admin dashboard with product & stock management |
| Orders via phone/WhatsApp, no tracking | Digital orders with status tracking (pending → delivered) |
| Customers ask for care details | Rich product descriptions & care info on detail page |
| No real-time stock visibility | Stock badges (In Stock / Low Stock / Out of Stock) |
| Cash + growing UPI usage | Cash on Delivery + UPI QR payment options |
| Limited reach of physical store | Online catalogue with search & filters |

### 4.3.2 Conclusion

The field visit confirmed that local plant retail faces significant operational challenges around **inventory visibility**, **order tracking**, and **customer reach**. The GreenLeaf Plants e-commerce system directly addresses these gaps by providing an online catalogue, automated order management with status tracking, live stock indicators, and flexible payment options — empowering the shop owner to manage the business through a simple admin dashboard while reaching a wider customer base.
